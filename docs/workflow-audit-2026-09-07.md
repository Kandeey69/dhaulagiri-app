# Daily-operation workflow audit — 7 September 2026

The current `MERGED APP` source contains **13 actionable discrepancies: 8 high priority (P1), 5 medium priority (P2)**. The main risks are incorrect carry-forward, changes lost during navigation, inconsistent inventory values, and partial database updates. Passing domain tests do not establish that these combined workflows are safe.

Scope: current working files, including pre-existing uncommitted changes, in `E:/Coding by Kandeey/Dhaulagiri App/MERGED APP`. Reviewed company creation/selection, fiscal-year creation and linking, closing/reopening, opening-balance refresh, party changes/deletion, transaction changes/deletion, stock valuation, persistence, and backup/restore. This was a source audit with isolated reproductions, not a live audit of the user's business balances or an installed-binary acceptance test. No production source or live business databases were changed.

## Findings, ordered by urgency

1. **[P1] Year-end can carry a year into itself and leave it unlocked.** In an unlinked FY 2082/83, enter `2082/83` as the next year. The handler only checks that the input is nonempty, then searches a list containing the current company. It selects the source as its own target. Carry-forward overwrites its opening with its closing, while retaining the transactions that generated that closing. For example, opening 100 plus sales 50 becomes opening 150 plus the same sales 50. The final target-profile upsert also overwrites the just-applied lock with the previously open profile. The isolated handler reproduction selected the same source/target ID, finished unlocked, and set its previous-year link to itself. Invalid codes are also accepted: `2083/99` produces dates for 2083/84, and unparseable codes silently become 2082/83. **Correction:** validate a canonical, consecutive fiscal-year code at setup and closing; require the exact successor year; exclude the source ID; reject cycles and incompatible existing links before any write. [Year selection](<E:/Coding by Kandeey/Dhaulagiri App/MERGED APP/src/App.tsx:2873>), [target selection](<E:/Coding by Kandeey/Dhaulagiri App/MERGED APP/src/App.tsx:2889>), [second profile write](<E:/Coding by Kandeey/Dhaulagiri App/MERGED APP/src/App.tsx:2943>), [date fallback](<E:/Coding by Kandeey/Dhaulagiri App/MERGED APP/src/domain/fiscalYear.ts:70>).

2. **[P1] Background opening refresh temporarily changes the company used by foreground work.** Returning to modules or logging out starts refresh without awaiting it. Refresh changes the global active company to the next year and performs asynchronous database work, while the displayed company remains the previous React state. Opening another module or starting another operation during this interval can resolve database URLs/settings using the temporary company. The final restoration can also undo a company selection made while refresh runs. The isolated helper reproduction confirms that the temporary target is globally visible throughout the await; a complete live misdirected write was not attempted. **Correction:** pass immutable company/database context into every operation; serialize refresh and navigation until those APIs are separated. [Unawaited refresh](<E:/Coding by Kandeey/Dhaulagiri App/MERGED APP/src/App.tsx:1676>), [global target switch](<E:/Coding by Kandeey/Dhaulagiri App/MERGED APP/src/App.tsx:248>), [temporary context helper](<E:/Coding by Kandeey/Dhaulagiri App/MERGED APP/src/App.tsx:419>).

3. **[P1] Saving a payment or party and immediately leaving can lose the change.** These handlers update React state, clear the form, and rely on a 600 ms autosave. Unmount cleanup cancels the timer without flushing it. Click Save and then Switch Module/Logout within that window: no database write is performed. Year-end refresh can consequently read the old payable balance. Party deletion additionally starts stock cleanup before the deferred financial save is durable. Executing the actual autosave effect and its cleanup produced zero save calls. **Correction:** await durable saves for explicit create/edit/delete actions and flush pending work before navigation, logout, refresh, or closing. [Autosave and cleanup](<E:/Coding by Kandeey/Dhaulagiri App/MERGED APP/src/purchase/App.tsx:1064>), [state-only write](<E:/Coding by Kandeey/Dhaulagiri App/MERGED APP/src/purchase/App.tsx:1426>), [payment save](<E:/Coding by Kandeey/Dhaulagiri App/MERGED APP/src/purchase/App.tsx:4024>).

4. **[P1] Stock summary/carry-forward and the detailed register disagree on closing value.** Summary and carry-forward use an average over all inward movements. The register costs each issue at the average immediately before that issue. Reproduction: open 10 units at NPR 100, sell all 10, then purchase 10 at NPR 200. Summary closes at NPR 1,500; the register closes at NPR 2,000. Carry-forward uses the summary calculation, so next-year opening disagrees with the previous-year register by NPR 500. **Correction:** select one costing policy and derive summary, register, exports, and carry-forward from the same valuation engine. [Summary valuation](<E:/Coding by Kandeey/Dhaulagiri App/MERGED APP/src/stock/services/stockLedger.ts:88>), [register valuation](<E:/Coding by Kandeey/Dhaulagiri App/MERGED APP/src/stock/services/stockLedger.ts:215>), [carry-forward calculation](<E:/Coding by Kandeey/Dhaulagiri App/MERGED APP/src/stock/services/stockCarryForward.ts:99>).

5. **[P1] Year-end succeeds with incomplete or mismatched inventory.** Carry-forward excludes mismatched stock documents, skips negative closing quantities, and does not stop for pending source documents without stock lines. It writes the remaining openings and reports `completed`. The year-end success text omits warnings when inventory status is completed, and then locks the source year. Reproduction: a posted sale with no stock lines still produced completed carry-forward, no warnings, and the full opening quantity of 10. **Correction:** require a readiness check for pending/mismatched documents, negative stock, and material item conflicts; show the affected documents and resolve them before locking. [Filtering/skipping](<E:/Coding by Kandeey/Dhaulagiri App/MERGED APP/src/stock/services/stockCarryForward.ts:87>), [unconditional completion](<E:/Coding by Kandeey/Dhaulagiri App/MERGED APP/src/stock/services/stockCarryForward.ts:191>), [success message](<E:/Coding by Kandeey/Dhaulagiri App/MERGED APP/src/App.tsx:2919>).

6. **[P1] Failed company deletion or replacement can leave only part of the company changed.** Company deletion clears accounts, then purchase, then stock. If a later step fails, the catch restores only the profile. In the isolated failure injection, the company remained visible and locked but its accounts data was already empty. Group deletion can similarly clear earlier years before a later year fails. Full-company backup replacement has the same gap: its catch restores profile metadata without restoring already-replaced databases. Individual accounts restore also deletes/reinserts records without a transaction. **Correction:** stage and validate replacements before activation; use database transactions plus recoverable snapshots/compensation for operations spanning stores, including settings and profiles. [Company clear](<E:/Coding by Kandeey/Dhaulagiri App/MERGED APP/src/App.tsx:1394>), [group deletion loop](<E:/Coding by Kandeey/Dhaulagiri App/MERGED APP/src/App.tsx:1412>), [replacement failure handler](<E:/Coding by Kandeey/Dhaulagiri App/MERGED APP/src/App.tsx:1185>).

7. **[P1] Sales and credit-note writes are not atomic with their journals.** Sale creation/update first persists the transaction, then removes/replaces ledger entries in separate statements. An error after the transaction write can leave the edited amount saved with no journal or an incomplete journal, even though the UI reports failure. An in-memory SQLite reproduction changed a sale to NPR 300, injected a ledger insertion failure, and observed the sale at NPR 300 with zero journal rows. Credit-note create/update uses the same pattern. **Correction:** place transaction record, journal replacement, dependent allocations, and audit record in one transaction, using the existing native transactional approach where appropriate. [Sale update](<E:/Coding by Kandeey/Dhaulagiri App/MERGED APP/src/accounts/data/storage.ts:1750>), [journal replacement](<E:/Coding by Kandeey/Dhaulagiri App/MERGED APP/src/accounts/data/storage.ts:908>), [credit-note posting](<E:/Coding by Kandeey/Dhaulagiri App/MERGED APP/src/accounts/data/storage.ts:2448>).

8. **[P1] SQLite initialization failure silently switches purchase work to a separate data store.** Any exception during SQLite repository creation returns a localStorage repository. Existing SQLite purchases can appear absent, while new entries save into localStorage. On a later successful startup, SQLite is selected again without merging those fallback changes. This also affects backup and closing, which call the same repository factory. This is source-traced; no live failure was induced. **Correction:** distinguish intentional browser storage from desktop database failure; keep desktop writes unavailable with a clear retry/recovery message until the expected database is open. [Fallback](<E:/Coding by Kandeey/Dhaulagiri App/MERGED APP/src/purchase/repository.ts:856>).

9. **[P2] Accounts backup/reset omits journal data and transaction lifecycle metadata.** Accounts backup contains parties, sales, collections, credit notes, allocations, and activity logs, but no ledger entries. Restore neither clears existing ledger entries nor rebuilds them. A new restored company therefore lacks these journals, while replacement/reset can retain journals for removed transactions. Restore INSERT statements also omit lifecycle fields, reverting to schema defaults instead of preserving the backed-up state. The statement-recording reproduction confirms that accounts reset deletes sales but never touches `ledger_entries`. **Correction:** version the backup schema to include journals and lifecycle fields, remap company/year IDs consistently, and restore them atomically. [Backup payload](<E:/Coding by Kandeey/Dhaulagiri App/MERGED APP/src/accounts/data/storage.ts:950>), [restore/reset](<E:/Coding by Kandeey/Dhaulagiri App/MERGED APP/src/accounts/data/storage.ts:986>), [sale restore columns](<E:/Coding by Kandeey/Dhaulagiri App/MERGED APP/src/accounts/data/storage.ts:1063>).

10. **[P2] Editing an allocated sale can leave over-allocation or cross-customer links.** Sale update changes the amount and party without checking existing receipt allocations. Reproduction: a NPR 1,000 sale allocated NPR 800 was changed to NPR 500 and another customer; the NPR 800 allocation remained attached. Customer-level outstanding and bill-level allocation data can then describe different situations. **Correction:** reject incompatible changes or explicitly unallocate/reallocate within the same transaction, checking both amount and customer identity. [Sale update](<E:/Coding by Kandeey/Dhaulagiri App/MERGED APP/src/accounts/data/storage.ts:1707>), [allocation creation rules](<E:/Coding by Kandeey/Dhaulagiri App/MERGED APP/src/accounts/data/storage.ts:790>).

11. **[P2] Refresh can retain obsolete opening balances after source changes.** An item previously carried with 10 units is later corrected to zero and marked inactive in the reopened source year. The planner now omits it, and the target upsert only updates supplied items, so the next year retains the old 10 units. Source item deletion/code changes have a related identity problem because stock carry-forward matches by code and stores no durable source-item association. For parties, a deleted source party with target-year activity is deliberately retained, but its old opening is not cleared. **Correction:** track opening provenance, retain masters required by target transactions, and explicitly reconcile obsolete carried openings to zero without deleting independent target-year data. The inactive-zero stock case was reproduced; the retained-party path was source-traced. [Skipped item](<E:/Coding by Kandeey/Dhaulagiri App/MERGED APP/src/stock/services/stockCarryForward.ts:124>), [upsert-only writer](<E:/Coding by Kandeey/Dhaulagiri App/MERGED APP/src/stock/storage.ts:1676>), [party retention](<E:/Coding by Kandeey/Dhaulagiri App/MERGED APP/src/application/carryForwardPartySync.ts:57>).

12. **[P2] Merely visiting the previous year can overwrite next-year opening edits and master changes.** Background refresh runs whenever the source has a next-year link, even if no source data changed. It unconditionally replaces target opening amounts and source-controlled names, addresses, active states, and stock fields. Example: edit a next-year opening from 100 to 120, inspect the previous year, and return to modules; refresh writes 100 back without an override decision. **Correction:** define which openings are derived versus manually adjustable, record overrides separately, and refresh only after committed source changes. Protect target-year master edits from unrelated source refresh. This is source-traced. [Refresh trigger](<E:/Coding by Kandeey/Dhaulagiri App/MERGED APP/src/App.tsx:1646>), [receivable overwrite](<E:/Coding by Kandeey/Dhaulagiri App/MERGED APP/src/accounts/data/storage.ts:1407>), [payable overwrite](<E:/Coding by Kandeey/Dhaulagiri App/MERGED APP/src/App.tsx:294>).

13. **[P2] A deleted seeded company returns after restart.** Startup merges the seed file into saved profiles every time. Deletion removes only the saved profile; it does not tombstone the seed identity. Consequently, a seeded company/year can reappear with its previously cleared databases. Startup also forces inventory tracking on for any seed whose flag is true, overriding a user's saved off setting. This applies only where a seed file exists; the isolated merge reproduces the deleted-profile resurrection. **Correction:** migrate seeds once, preserve explicit user settings, and persist deleted-seed identities. ## Decisions to settle during correction

- Reopening an older year while its successor is locked currently allows source edits, but the successor's write guard prevents refreshing its opening. Choose an explicit policy: reopen/reconcile dependent years together, or record corrections in an open year. Do not imply propagation succeeded merely because the original edit saved.
- The domain lifecycle says posted financial fields are immutable and corrections use reversal; several operational screens intentionally allow direct edits/deletes of posted transactions in open years. Align the chosen operational policy, audit-history requirements, storage enforcement, and tests.
- Sales/collections and purchase/payments maintain separate party masters. Company carry-forward preserves them separately. Any expectation that adding/editing a party in one module updates the other needs an explicit identity/synchronization design.

Recommended correction order: prevent self-carry and global company races; make explicit saves durable; establish one inventory valuation/readiness rule; make destructive and financial writes atomic/recoverable; then repair backup completeness and opening/allocation reconciliation. Re-run the affected workflows across at least three linked years, including reopening the oldest year, target-year activity, deletion, restart, and injected persistence failures.
